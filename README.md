# Shri R.K. Coaching Centre — Official Demo Website

> **Designed & Developed by Tech Ishant** ⚡

A modern, high-converting, mobile-first responsive landing page built for **Shri R.K. Coaching Centre**, located in Shanti Nagar / Veer Sambhaji Nagar, Dahisar East, Mumbai.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- 📱 **Mobile-First & Ultra Fast**: Lightweight semantic HTML5 & CSS with zero bloated JS dependencies.
- 📸 **Actual Center Photos**: Integrated store and classroom images of Shri R.K. Coaching Centre.
- 🎯 **Targeted Academic Programs**:
  - Primary Foundation (Class 1 to 5)
  - Secondary / Board Mastery (Class 9 & 10 SSC)
  - Information Technology (IT) & Practical Computer Literacy
- 💬 **One-Click WhatsApp Lead Generator**: Interactive demo booking form that automatically formats and sends student admission details directly to the academy's WhatsApp (+91 86522 92021).
- 📍 **Local Dahisar East Context**: Complete address, landmark directions (Opposite SVP School & Near Dahisar Metro), and direct Google Maps navigation button.
- ⭐ **4.9/5 Star Rating Showcase**: Parent & student testimonials from local Dahisar families.
- 📞 **Sticky Mobile Action Bar**: Instant call, WhatsApp enquiry, and map directions.
